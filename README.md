
# Towards a FAIR and open methodology at NIME [working title]

## Abstract

With a NIME 2026 theme of communities this paper looks at how NIME papers could 
engage with a wider community by aligning with the FAIR principles
of findable accesible, interoperable and reusable research.
the paper covers the previous work that has looked at open source practice
and ways to inprove it at nime. this is contextualise with an audit of open source
practice at the NIME 2025. In light of what has previously been written and pruposed
towards open source practice, an open methodology for digital musical instruments is 
discussed through the lens of the open sourc and archival practoce of a recent NIME project.
this is concluded with recommendations on how the NIME community could adapt its 
practice and hopefully ensure longevity and discoverablility of NIME projects and thus
lowering easing the waybfor new communities to engage with the work at nime.
