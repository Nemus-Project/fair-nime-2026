# Informal summary

Paper on NIME digital sustainability: @cite{west_towards_2025,fiordelmondo_towards_2025,astrid_towards_2021} and mentions of @cite{mcpherson_nimehub_2016}

In the spirit of the theme of community this is a short paper looking at digital sustainability practice in NIME, what has been discussed so far, what progress has been made, the development of these methodologies with reference to the approach taken for the harpsichord keyboard project at NEMUS.

The approach was to separate the project into separate modules, one each for hardware, firmware, and 3D models / measurment data. This is not prescriptive, the central takeaway is that an instryment is not one part but rather a collection of part that cohere together, the idea to permit reuse of an ideas and allow for development of individual modules separate to the instrument.

There is also of "best" and "good-enough" practice. There is still systematic guidance missing. There is also call for making the submission of archives a more normal practice and reduce the friction in doing so.

At the moment, NIME papers are archived, but accompanying archive of software / firmware / documentation / CAD models / bill of material is largely absent.
